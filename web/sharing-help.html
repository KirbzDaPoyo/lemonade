LEMONADESharing help

A little help with your shortlist.

My link will not open

Ask the person who sent it for their current link. They may have disabled or replaced it, removed the plan, or still need to add public names. No account is needed to view a working link.

The page could not load

Check your internet connection and refresh. If sharing is busy, wait a minute before trying again. If the problem continues, ask the sender to check their sharing status in Lemonade.

A place looks wrong

Names and locations are written by the sender. Ask them to correct the public details. Google Maps opens separately and may show a different or unavailable destination.

Who can see this?

Anyone with the link can view and forward the shortlist. The page is read-only. Private notes, visit history and account details are not included automatically. The owner can stop future access, but cannot remove screenshots or copies already made.

I own the plan

In Lemonade, open your outing plan and choose Manage read-only sharing. You can edit its public details, preview it, disable its link or replace the link. For account help, see account access and deletion instructions.
